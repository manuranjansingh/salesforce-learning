# Salesforce Fundamentals

## What is Salesforce?

Salesforce is a cloud-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform that helps organizations manage sales, customer service, marketing, and business processes.

## What is CRM?

CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a strategy and system used to manage customer interactions, improve relationships, and increase business growth.

## Salesforce Architecture Basics

Salesforce follows a multi-tenant architecture where multiple organizations share the same infrastructure while keeping data secure and isolated.

Key Components:
- Objects
- Records
- Fields
- Apps
- Users

## Standard vs Custom Objects

### Standard Objects
- Account
- Contact
- Lead
- Opportunity
- Case

### Custom Objects

Custom Objects are created by organizations to store business-specific information.

## Profiles vs Roles

### Profiles

Profiles control:
- Object permissions
- Field permissions
- App access
- User permissions

### Roles

Roles control:
- Record visibility
- Data access through role hierarchy

## Summary

Salesforce is a leading CRM platform that uses objects, users, profiles, and roles to manage business data securely and efficiently.
