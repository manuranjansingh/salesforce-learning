# Sharing Rules

## Introduction

Sharing Rules extend record access to users beyond the default organization-wide settings.

They provide additional access to records.

---

## Why Use Sharing Rules?

Benefits:

- Increase collaboration
- Share records automatically
- Reduce manual sharing

---

## Types of Sharing Rules

### Owner-Based Sharing Rules

Share records owned by certain users.

Example:

Share all Opportunities owned by Sales Team with Marketing Team.

---

### Criteria-Based Sharing Rules

Share records based on field values.

Example:

Share Cases where Priority = High.

---

## Relationship with OWD

Organization-Wide Defaults (OWD) define baseline access.

Sharing Rules can only open access further.

They cannot make access more restrictive.

---

## Example

OWD:

Private

Sharing Rule:

Share all Accounts owned by Sales Team with Support Team.

Result:

Support Team gains visibility.

---

## Best Practices

- Start with restrictive OWD
- Use sharing rules only when needed
- Avoid excessive sharing rules

---

## Summary

Sharing Rules provide additional record access beyond Organization-Wide Defaults and improve collaboration across teams.
