# Organization & Company Settings

## Introduction

Organization and Company Settings define the basic configuration of a Salesforce org.

These settings help organizations manage users, business processes, time zones, currencies, and working schedules.

---

## Company Information

Company Information contains basic details about the Salesforce organization.

Information includes:

* Organization Name
* Organization ID
* Salesforce Edition
* Instance Name
* Default Language

Example:

A company can view its Salesforce edition and organization details from Company Information.

---

## Fiscal Year

A Fiscal Year is used for financial planning and reporting.

Types:

* Standard Fiscal Year
* Custom Fiscal Year

Benefits:

* Revenue tracking
* Financial reporting
* Sales forecasting

Example:

A company may use April–March as its fiscal year instead of January–December.

---

## Business Hours

Business Hours define the organization's working schedule.

Example:

Monday to Friday

* Start: 9:00 AM
* End: 6:00 PM

Uses:

* Case Escalation
* Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
* Support Operations

---

## Holidays

Holidays define non-working days.

Examples:

* Independence Day
* Diwali
* Christmas

Benefits:

* Accurate SLA calculations
* Better support scheduling

---

## Language and Locale Settings

These settings determine how information is displayed to users.

Examples:

* Date format
* Time format
* Number format
* Language

Example:

India:

* Date: DD/MM/YYYY

United States:

* Date: MM/DD/YYYY

---

## Time Zone Settings

Time Zones ensure that users see data according to their geographic location.

Example:

* India Standard Time (IST)
* Pacific Standard Time (PST)

Benefits:

* Accurate scheduling
* Better collaboration across regions

---

## Currency Management

Currency settings help organizations manage global business transactions.

Features:

* Single Currency
* Multiple Currencies
* Advanced Currency Management

Example:

A company operating in India and the USA can use INR and USD.

---

## Why Company Settings Matter

Company Settings help organizations:

* Maintain consistency
* Improve reporting accuracy
* Manage global operations
* Support business processes

---

## Summary

Organization and Company Settings provide the foundation of a Salesforce org by managing company information, fiscal years, business hours, holidays, time zones, languages, and currencies.
